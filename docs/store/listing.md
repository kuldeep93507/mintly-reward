## Full description (max 4000)
Roll the dice and race your tokens home in Ludo Mintly — the classic board game, fresh and colourful!

GAME MODES
• Play Online — quick 2 or 4 player matches with players online
• Play with Friends — create a private room and share the room code
• Vs Computer — Easy, Normal or Hard computer players, works offline
• Pass & Play — 2 to 4 players on one phone
• Snakes & Ladders — climb the ladders, dodge the snakes

FEATURES
• Classic Ludo rules: roll a 6 to open, capture to go again, safe star squares
• Smooth animations, sounds and vibration
• Emojis and quick phrases during online games
• Daily rewards, levels and a leaderboard
• Board themes and dice skins
• Break reminder after an hour of play
• No ads

FREE COINS ONLY
Coins in Ludo Mintly are free virtual coins used only for fun. They cannot be bought, and they cannot be
exchanged for money, gift cards or any real-world prize. There is no real-money play.

FAIR PLAY
In online matches the dice are rolled by our game server at random.

Ludo Mintly is for players aged 13 and older.
