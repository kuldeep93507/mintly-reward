Ludo Mintly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what the Ludo Mintly app ("the app", "we") collects and why. Contact: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • A random device ID that the app creates on first launch, used to give you a guest account without asking for an email or phone number.
  • Your nickname and chosen avatar, shown to other players.
  • Game data: coin balance and history, games played, wins, level, and the moves of online matches while they are running.
  • Player ID and recent players: a short public Player ID so friends can invite you, and a list of players you recently played online with (so you can invite each other again).
  • Offline game summaries: while the app is connected to our server, it sends the current board of offline games (vs computer, pass & play, snakes & ladders) so the game operator can see and manage them (see Fair play). Nothing is sent when you are not connected, and summaries are kept only in memory for a few minutes.
  • Technical data: your IP address is seen by our server while you are connected (standard for any internet connection) and may appear in short-lived server logs.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or payment details. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run the game: keep your profile and coins, match you with other players, run online matches, show leaderboards, prevent cheating and abuse, and answer your support requests.

Fair play

In online matches against other players, the dice are rolled by our server at random and the result is not changed by anyone. In offline games that run on your own device (vs computer, pass & play, snakes & ladders), the game operator can view the board and may adjust the dice, for example for testing or support. No coins are won or lost in offline games.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Other players see your nickname, avatar, level and in-game reactions. Our server is hosted by a cloud provider that processes data on our behalf.

Coins

Coins are free virtual items with no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or rewards.

Retention and account deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, nickname, Player ID, coins, coin history, stats and recent-player list.
  • Without the app: email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my account" and your nickname or Player ID (shown in the app under Profile). We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Short-lived server logs (which may contain IP addresses) are kept for at most 30 days.

Security

Data is sent to our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Age

The app is for players aged 13 and older and asks for your age on first launch. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Healthy play

The app offers a reminder to take a break after an hour of play (Settings → Break reminder). There are no purchases, no cash prizes and no way to exchange coins for anything of value.

Contact and grievances

For questions, complaints, reporting another player, or data requests, use Settings → Contact us / Report a problem in the app, or email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
